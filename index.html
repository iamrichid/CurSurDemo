Live Playground — Quote by address

Send pickup and drop-off addresses — ANY3MI geocodes, routes, and prices the trip in Ghana Cedis. The interactive playground lets you pick bicycle, motorbike (okada), or car and quote East Legon, Accra → Kwame Nkrumah Circle, Accra. 

curl -X POST https://api.any3mi.com/v1/quote \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer a3_live_sk_abc123" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "origin": { "address": "East Legon, Accra" },
    "destination": { "address": "Kwame Nkrumah Circle, Accra" },
    "vehicle": "motorbike"
  }'

Example response: GH₵ 29.12 · 8.4 km · 24 min · Motorbike (Okada).
